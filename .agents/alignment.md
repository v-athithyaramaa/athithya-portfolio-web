## Design System: Architectural Brutalism
- **Core Aesthetic**: Raw materials, exposed structure, anti-frivolity paired with blueprint schematics.
- **Layout Patterns**: Strict, 1px bordered Mechanical Bento Grids. Horizontal & Non-Linear Flows.
- **Dark Mode Palette**:
  - Background: Deep Blueprint Navy (`#000a12`)
  - Grid Lines & Borders: Faded Cyan (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)`)
  - Text & Accents: Ghost White (`#e2e8f0`) / Neon Blueprint Blue (`#00F0FF`).
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (Primary/Structural) and JetBrains Mono (Secondary/Data).
- **Micro-interactions**: Crosshair cursor, CAD-like hover states, snappy linear easing.
